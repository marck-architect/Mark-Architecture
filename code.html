How does the 1-on-1 online architectural consultation work?

remove
Once you select your session (30-min Audit for PKR 3,000 or 60-min Masterclass for PKR 5,000) and complete secure checkout via Safepay, you receive an automated confirmation with your Google Meet link. You can upload floor plans or site photos for the architect to review live during the call.

Do you guarantee approval by CDA, LDA, DHA, and PDA?

add
Yes. All drawings are signed and stamped by PCATP licensed architects (Reg. #A-05842) and formatted strictly according to the statutory bylaws of your local development authority (CDA Islamabad, LDA Lahore, DHA Nationwide, PDA Peshawar, RDA Rawalpindi, and KDA Karachi).

What deliverables are included in the PKR 57/sq.ft turnkey package?

add
The complete package includes Architectural Working Drawings, 3D Photorealistic Exterior Elevations, Structural Engineering calculations & reinforcement schedules, Plumbing & Sanitary schematics, Electrical conduit & switchboard layouts, and Authority Submission Sheets.

What is the 50% advance milestone policy?

add
To initiate architectural drafting and allocate studio engineering resources, a 50% advance commitment is required through Safepay. The remaining 50% balance is payable upon deliverable verification before final high-resolution print files and submission sets are dispatched.

Can Overseas Pakistanis work with MARK Architects remotely?

add
Extensively. Over 40% of our residential commissions are for overseas clients based in the UK, UAE, USA, and Saudi Arabia constructing homes in Pakistan. We conduct milestone reviews via video conference and provide digital cloud archives of all drawing sheets.
